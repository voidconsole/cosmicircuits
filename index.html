01 // Mission Overview

Bengaluru's 2-day hardware mission.

Part of Hack Club Blueprint's global Build Guilds week running April 13–19 across 100+ cities. Every guild is local. Every build is real.

01

The Audience

Any teen aged 13–18. Zero hardware experience required. Bring curiosity and a phone. Beginners and serial makers both welcome.

02

The Space

A curated, hardware friendly space in Bengaluru. Venue confirmed closer to the event sign up to get the details direct.

03

The Build

Workshops, project demos, lightning talks, soldering sessions, open build time. Makers colliding at full speed for one full day.

04

The Cost

Completely free. Hack Club funds each guild per attendee: food, venue, and materials included. You bring the ideas.

05

The Scale

100+ guilds running globally the same week. Same energy, same mission. Bengaluru on the map alongside every other city.

06

Blueprint

Hack Club's hardware program, built with AMD. Real parts, real guidance, and genuine support for teen makers building ambitious projects.

02 // Destination

Where we're headed.

Bengaluru is just the launchpad. Cosmic Circuits is part of a global wave of builders from over 100 cities, one shared week, all making something real. Whether you're soldering your first circuit or shipping your tenth project, this is where the next chapter starts. The road ahead is wide open.
